Connect tools to a calmer home.

This console connects to HomeRelay’s self-hosted MCP server, discovers its tools, and calls them through JSON-RPC. Approve a plan only after reviewing the actions. The included planner is rule-based demo logic—not a live LLM or a direct Alexa+ account connection.

Demo data only: the server does not send real notifications, purchase items, or control physical devices. Its in-memory state may reset when the hosting service restarts.

1 · Connect to the MCP endpoint

Paste the deployed service URL ending in /mcp. For local testing, use http://localhost:10000/mcp.
